Frequently Asked Inquiries

Can I request video inspection before dispatch?

Yes, absolutely. Once your saree is reserved, simply message us on WhatsApp at +91 84988 54323 or +91 81067 89789 and our team will share a 4K drape video or schedule a live video call.

How do I know my saree is authentic Silk Mark?

Every silk saree carries a verifiable Silk Mark tag backed by the Central Silk Board of India with a unique holographic security code.

What is your return & exchange window?

We provide a seamless 7-day hassle-free return and exchange policy from the day of delivery with complimentary insured return pickup across India.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship globally with express air couriers (DHL / FedEx) reaching the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, and Australia in 3 to 6 business days.
