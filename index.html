Craft & Heritage

The Art of the Drape

“Rooted in Indian craftsmanship and imagined for the modern woman, PALLUVO celebrates the quiet magic of getting dressed.”

We partner directly with traditional weaving clusters across Varanasi, Kanchipuram, and Chanderi. Every saree is woven with unhurried patience on wooden pit-looms, using certified pure silk yarns, authentic tested zari, and organic botanical dyes. Designed not just to be worn, but to be treasured as an heirloom.
