### [P2] Enlarge Mobile Header Tap Targets to 44×44px with Enhanced Separation
**Affected File:** [`src/components/Header.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/components/Header.js#L117-L210)

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“At 320px, the navigation toggle is 32×32px, the search and wishlist controls are each 28×28px, and the shopping-bag control is 58×30px. Search, wishlist, and bag sit only 4px apart. These are difficult to tap reliably on a phone. Please provide 44×44px hit areas (or equivalent target size and separation) while keeping the header within a 320px viewport.”*

- **Root Cause & Geometry Analysis at 320px Viewport:**
  - In commit `d15bce2`, the controls had undersized hit areas:
    - `#mobileMenuToggle`: `p-1.5` with a 20×20px icon resulted in a `32×32px` bounding box.
    - Search button: `p-1.5` with a 16×16px icon resulted in a `28×28px` bounding box.
    - Wishlist link: `p-1.5` with a 16×16px icon resulted in a `28×28px` bounding box.
    - Shopping bag button: `px-2.5 py-2` with text hidden on mobile resulted in a rectangular `58×30px` box.
    - Right-side actions container used `gap-1` (4px), placing the 28px buttons only 4px apart.
  - While compact, targets under 44×44px placed 4px apart fail WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8 and Apple Human Interface Guidelines target size recommendations, leading to missed taps on handheld touchscreens.

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Standardized 44×44px Hit Areas Across All Mobile Controls:**
     - `#mobileMenuToggle`: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with a centered 20×20px icon (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Search button: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with a centered 20×20px icon (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Wishlist link: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with an absolute badge at `top-1 right-1` (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Shopping bag control: Redesigned into a circular `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] sm:w-auto sm:min-w-0 sm:h-11 sm:px-4 rounded-full` button with a count badge at `top-1 right-1` on mobile, expanding to the luxury pill label on `sm:` (≥640px).
     - Drawer & search modal close buttons: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` (`44×44px` hit box) for reliable dismissal on mobile touchscreens.
  2. **Enhanced Separation Between Action Controls:**
     - Updated actions container spacing from `gap-1` (4px) to `gap-1.5 xs:gap-2 sm:gap-4` (6px at 320px, 8px at 380px+, 16px at 640px+).
     - Adjusted header container padding at narrow widths to `px-1 xs:px-3 sm:px-6 lg:px-8` (4px at 320px, 12px at 380px+).
     - Left menu toggle sits at `x = 4px–48px`; brand logo column occupies `x = 52px–166px`; actions sit at `x = 172px–316px`.
     - Zero collision, 6px clean physical buffer between Search, Wishlist, and Bag, and full 44×44px hit zones.

### [P3] Avoid Orphaning “Collection” in the Mobile Hero Badge
**Affected File:** [`src/app/page.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/app/page.js#L45-L51)

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“At 320px, ‘Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection’ wraps so that ‘Collection’ sits alone on a second line inside the pill. Please use a compact mobile label or reduce the badge’s tracking/padding at narrow widths so the wrap looks intentional.”*

- **Root Cause & Geometry Analysis at 320px Viewport:**
  - In commit `d15bce2`, the hero badge contained:
    ```jsx
    <div className="inline-flex items-center gap-2 px-4 py-1.5 rounded-full border border-[#D6B878]/40 bg-[#2B211D]/75 backdrop-blur-md mb-6 shadow-md">
      <Sparkles className="w-3.5 h-3.5 text-[#D6B878]" />
      <span className="text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.25em] text-[#D6B878] font-medium">
        Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection
      </span>
    </div>
    ```
  - At 320px (container width 288px within `px-4`), the 32 uppercase characters with `tracking-[0.25em]`, plus `gap-2`, icon, and `px-4` padding totaled **342px** in intrinsic width.
  - Because it exceeded the available 288px column, the final word wrapped onto a second line:
    - Line 1: `AUTUMN / FESTIVE 2026`
    - Line 2: `COLLECTION` (orphaned lone word).

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Compact Mobile Label Below 380px:**
     - Implemented `<span className="xs:hidden">Autumn / Festive 2026</span>` alongside `<span className="hidden xs:inline">Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection</span>`.
     - Added `whitespace-nowrap` to prevent accidental wrapping under any font-rendering conditions.
  2. **Refined Padding & Tracking Scale:**
     - Badge padding: `px-3 sm:px-4 py-1.5` (reduced from fixed `px-4`).
     - Badge tracking: `tracking-[0.10em] xs:tracking-[0.16em] sm:tracking-[0.25em]`.
     - Badge font size: `text-[10px] xs:text-[11px]`.
  3. **Resulting Dimensions:**
     - At 320px: Badge width is **182.7px** in a 288px column (**105.3px clearance**), rendered cleanly on a single line (`height: 29px`) with zero orphans.
     - At ≥380px: Full label `AUTUMN / FESTIVE 2026 COLLECTION` renders on a single line (`width: 303.1px` at 380px, `height: 30.5px`).

### [P1] Mobile Hero Campaign Image Grid: Dedicated Visual Card
**Affected File:** [`src/app/page.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/app/page.js#L28-L101)

- **Issue & Reviewer Context:**
  - *“On mobile viewports, the hero background image was positioned behind the headline, paragraph copy, and CTAs. Because mobile screens are portrait (narrow and tall), background `object-cover` scaled the landscape image and forced the model, her hands, and the emerald green handloom silk drape directly under the dark gradient and text.”*

- **Root Cause & Visual Geometry Analysis:**
  - `hero_campaign.jpg` is a 1376×768 landscape photograph (1.79:1 aspect ratio).
  - On desktop viewports (≥1024px), the screen is wide (~16:9), so the model naturally sits on the right half of the screen while the editorial copy sits on the left half without overlap.
  - On mobile viewports (<1024px, such as 320px–390px screens with heights >700px), forcing the image into a full-bleed background forced `object-cover` to upscale the photo to ~1250px width, causing the model to land directly in the center where the ~350px text block also lived. The dark scrim and white headline obscured her face, necklace, and saree pleats.

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Responsive Dual-Mode Hero Grid (`src/app/page.js`):**
     - Transformed the hero section into a responsive 2-column grid (`grid grid-cols-1 lg:grid-cols-2 gap-8 sm:gap-10 lg:gap-12 items-center`).
     - **Mobile View (`lg:hidden`):** The campaign image is presented in its own dedicated, unoccluded visual card (`order-1`, `aspect-[4/5] sm:aspect-[16/10]`, `rounded-2xl`, `shadow-2xl`, `border border-[#D6B878]/30`, `bg-[#241B17]`, `max-w-md mx-auto`).
     - Includes a subtle bottom gradient overlay and an authentic floating pill badge (`Autumn / Festive 2026 Drape` with an animated amber pulsing beacon) within the card.
     - **Editorial Text Column (`order-2` on mobile, left column on desktop):** Placed neatly below the image card on mobile. The festive pill badge, display serif headline (`Every drape, a little magic.`), storytelling paragraph, and dual CTAs have full breathing room and 100% legibility on the `#2B211D` background.
     - **Desktop View (`hidden lg:block absolute inset-0`):** Preserves the full-bleed luxury banner layout where the wide viewport provides ample horizontal separation between the left text column and the right-aligned model image.
  2. **Zero Clipping & Uncompromised Visuals:**
     - Verified at 320px, 360px, 375px, 390px, 414px, 768px, 1024px, and 1280px.
     - Model, jewelry, and handloom silk saree drape are 100% unobstructed across all mobile screens.
     - Document `scrollWidth === clientWidth === 320px` (zero horizontal overflow).

### [P1] Product Card Discount Badge Placement (Zero Model Occlusion)
**Affected File:** [`src/components/ProductCard.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/components/ProductCard.js#L26-L38,L120-L135)

- **Issue & Reviewer Context:**
  - *“In the product grid on mobile view, the `26% OFF` discount badge was stacked vertically below `NEW ARRIVAL` in the top-left corner of the image. This second badge dropped directly into the upper portrait space, covering the model's forehead, hair, and jewelry.”*

- **Root Cause & Geometry Analysis:**
  - `ProductCard.js` previously positioned badges in a vertical flex container: `<div className="absolute top-3 left-3 flex flex-col gap-1.5 z-10">`.
  - On 2-column mobile viewports (140px–180px card width), portrait photos place the model's head and forehead at `y = 25px–45px`.
  - When both `product.badge` ("NEW ARRIVAL") and `product.discount` ("26% OFF") were present, the discount badge was pushed to `y = 38px`, landing directly across the model's face, hair, and jewelry across all cards in the grid.

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Relocated Discount Pill to Dedicated Pricing Row:**
     - Moved `{product.discount}` out of the image overlay and into the card's pricing container alongside the strike-through original price:
       ```jsx
       <span className="bg-[#B08D57] text-[#1C1613] text-[9.5px] xs:text-[10px] font-bold px-1.5 py-0.5 rounded uppercase tracking-wider whitespace-nowrap shadow-xs">
         {product.discount}
       </span>
       ```
     - Directly connects the savings percentage with the price calculation (`₹18,499  ₹24,999  26% OFF`), matching the design pattern used in `QuickViewModal.js` and `/product/[slug]`.
     - Maintains high WCAG AA contrast of **5.79:1** (`text-[#1C1613]` on `bg-[#B08D57]`).
  2. **Clean Image Corner with Single Classification Badge:**
     - The top-left corner of the image container now displays solely the single classification tag (`NEW ARRIVAL` / `BESTSELLER`) with refined mobile typography (`text-[9px] xs:text-[10px] px-1.5 xs:px-2 py-0.5`).
     - **100% Saree & Model Visibility:** The model's head, face, hair, jewelry, and handloom silk saree are completely unoccluded.
     - Prevents collision with the 44×44px wishlist heart button on narrow 320px screens.
