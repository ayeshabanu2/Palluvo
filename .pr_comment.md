### [P2] Separate the Artisan-Hours Label from the Explore Action
**Affected File:** `src/app/page.js` (lines 151–160), `src/data/products.js`, `js/products.js`

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“In src/app/page.js:151–157, the footer row has no minimum gap or narrow-screen stacking rule. On the Banarasi card, the measured text boxes touch: ‘210+ · Weaving Hours’ ends exactly where ‘Explore’ begins, so the row reads visually as WeavingEXPLORE. Please preserve clear separation by adding a gap, allowing the action to wrap/stack, or simplifying the mobile label; verify the narrowest supported width.”*

- **Root Cause & Layout Analysis:**
  - On a 320px screen, the 2-column signature grid (`gap-3.5`) inside a 288px container allocates **137px** per card. With `p-3` (12px) padding, the footer row has an inner width of **113px**.
  - Previously, `flex items-center justify-between` had no minimum `gap` and no wrapping rule. With the full string `"210+ Weaving Hours"` (~99px) plus `"Explore"` (~48px), the contents totaled ~147px, causing the right edge of the artisan hours text box to touch the left edge of the Explore action (`WeavingEXPLORE`).

- **Resolution:**
  1. **Added Minimum Gap & Stacking Rule:** In `src/app/page.js`, updated the footer container to:
     ```jsx
     <div className="flex flex-wrap sm:flex-nowrap items-center justify-between gap-x-2 gap-y-1.5 text-[10px] sm:text-xs pt-2.5 sm:pt-3 border-t border-[#EDE3D5] text-[#2B211D]">
       <span className="text-[#665E57] font-semibold flex items-center gap-1.5 shrink-0">
         <span className="w-1.5 h-1.5 rounded-full bg-[#B08D57] shrink-0" aria-hidden="true" />
         <span className="sm:hidden">{model.shortArtisanHours || model.artisanHours}</span>
         <span className="hidden sm:inline">{model.artisanHours}</span>
       </span>
       <span className="font-semibold uppercase tracking-wider text-[#641C2D] group-hover:translate-x-1 transition-transform flex items-center gap-0.5 sm:gap-1 shrink-0 ml-auto sm:ml-0">
         Explore <span className="hidden sm:inline">Drapes</span> <ArrowRight className="w-3 h-3 sm:w-3.5 sm:h-3.5" />
       </span>
     </div>
     ```
  2. **Concise Mobile Hours (`shortArtisanHours`):** Added concise labels in `PALLUVO_TOP_MODELS` (e.g. `"210+ hrs"`, `"180+ hrs"`, `"Tailored"`).
  3. **Stacking & Alignment Behavior:**
     - With `shrink-0` and `flex-wrap`, when wrapped on narrow viewports, the Explore action cleanly breaks to row 2 and aligns to the right edge via `ml-auto sm:ml-0`, maintaining a dedicated **6px vertical gap** (`gap-y-1.5`).
     - On wider viewports (`sm:` and above), the row displays full artisan text (`"210+ Weaving Hours"`) single-line with standard spacing.

- **Verified In-Browser Measurements (320px Viewport):**
  - **Banarasi Card:** `artisanText: "210+ hrs"` (width: 52px), `exploreText: "EXPLORE →"` (width: 63px).
  - **Stacking State:** Stacked with `verticalGap: 6px` — zero overlap, zero text collision.

### [P2] Keep Long Region Labels from Crowding the Model-Card Overlay
**Affected File:** `src/app/page.js` (lines 134–146), `src/data/products.js`, `js/products.js`

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“In src/app/page.js:135–141, the 9px region label wraps without a line limit. At 320px, labels such as ‘Bhoodan Pochampally, Telangana’ and ‘Palluvo Contemporary Atelier’ take three lines; together with the model name and subtitle, the overlay occupies over half the card image and becomes visually cramped. Please use a concise mobile label or otherwise preserve the title/subtitle hierarchy at narrow widths.”*

- **Root Cause & Layout Analysis:**
  - In a 137px wide card with 4:5 aspect ratio, image height is **169px**.
  - Long region labels (e.g., `"Bhoodan Pochampally, Telangana"` [30 chars] and `"PALLUVO Contemporary Atelier"` [28 chars]) in uppercase tracking-wider wrapped across 3 lines (~36px). Combined with model name (20px) and 2-line subtitle (28px), overlay text occupied >96px (>56% of image height), obscuring over half the saree photography.

- **Resolution:**
  1. **Concise Mobile Region (`shortRegion`):** Added concise region identifiers in `PALLUVO_TOP_MODELS`:
     - `"Bhoodan Pochampally, Telangana"` $\rightarrow$ `"Pochampally"` on mobile (`sm:hidden`)
     - `"PALLUVO Contemporary Atelier"` $\rightarrow$ `"Atelier"` on mobile (`sm:hidden`)
     - `"PALLUVO Signature Studio"` $\rightarrow$ `"Studio"` on mobile (`sm:hidden`)
     - `"Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh"` $\rightarrow$ `"Varanasi"`
     - `"Paithan & Yeola, Maharashtra"` $\rightarrow$ `"Paithan"`
     - `"Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu"` $\rightarrow$ `"Kanchipuram"`
     - `"Chanderi, Madhya Pradesh"` $\rightarrow$ `"Chanderi"`
     - `"Mysuru, Karnataka"` $\rightarrow$ `"Mysuru"`
  2. **Single-Line Truncation:** Added `truncate block` to the region span to enforce a strict single-line limit.
  3. **Preserved Hierarchy:** Set subtitle to `line-clamp-1 sm:line-clamp-2` at narrow widths:
     ```jsx
     <div className="absolute bottom-2.5 left-2.5 right-2.5 sm:bottom-4 sm:left-4 sm:right-4 text-white">
       <span className="text-[9px] sm:text-[11px] uppercase tracking-wider text-[#D6B878] font-medium block truncate">
         <span className="sm:hidden">{model.shortRegion || model.region}</span>
         <span className="hidden sm:inline">{model.region}</span>
       </span>
       <h3 className="font-serif text-base sm:text-2xl font-bold mt-0.5 sm:mt-1 text-white leading-tight line-clamp-1">
         {model.name}
       </h3>
       <p className="text-[10px] sm:text-xs text-[#EDE3D5]/90 line-clamp-1 sm:line-clamp-2 mt-0.5 font-light leading-snug">
         {model.subtitle}
       </p>
     </div>
     ```

- **Verified In-Browser Measurements (320px Viewport):**
  - **Region Label Height:** Exactly **14px** across all 8 cards (strictly 1 single line).
  - **Subtitle Height:** Exactly **14px** (strictly 1 single line).
  - **Total Overlay Height:** **51px** on a 169px image container.
  - **Overlay Proportion:** **30.4%** of card image (reduced from >56%), preserving ~70% unobstructed view of the authentic handloom drape.
