### [P2] Enlarge Mobile Header Tap Targets to 44×44px with Enhanced Separation
**Affected File:** [`src/components/Header.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/components/Header.js#L117-L210)

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“At 320px, the navigation toggle is 32×32px, the search and wishlist controls are each 28×28px, and the shopping-bag control is 58×30px. Search, wishlist, and bag sit only 4px apart. These are difficult to tap reliably on a phone. Please provide 44×44px hit areas (or equivalent target size and separation) while keeping the header within a 320px viewport.”*

- **Root Cause & Geometry Analysis at 320px Viewport:**
  - In commit `d15bce2`, the controls had undersized hit areas:
    - `#mobileMenuToggle`: `p-1.5` with a 20×20px icon resulted in a `32×32px` bounding box.
    - Search button: `p-1.5` with a 16×16px icon resulted in a `28×28px` bounding box.
    - Wishlist link: `p-1.5` with a 16×16px icon resulted in a `28×28px` bounding box.
    - Shopping bag button: `px-2.5 py-2` with text hidden on mobile resulted in a rectangular `58×30px` box.
    - Right-side actions container used `gap-1` (4px), placing the 28px buttons only 4px apart.
  - While compact, targets under 44×44px placed 4px apart fail WCAG 2.5.5 / 2.5.8 and Apple Human Interface Guidelines target size recommendations, leading to missed taps on handheld touchscreens.

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Standardized 44×44px Hit Areas Across All Mobile Controls:**
     - `#mobileMenuToggle`: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with a centered 20×20px icon (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Search button: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with a centered 20×20px icon (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Wishlist link: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` with an absolute badge at `top-1 right-1` (`44×44px` hit box).
     - Shopping bag control: Redesigned into a circular `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] sm:w-auto sm:min-w-0 sm:h-11 sm:px-4 rounded-full` button with a count badge at `top-1 right-1` on mobile, expanding to the luxury pill label on `sm:` (≥640px).
     - Drawer & search modal close buttons: Assigned `w-11 h-11 min-w-[44px] min-h-[44px] rounded-full flex items-center justify-center` (`44×44px` hit box) for reliable dismissal on mobile touchscreens.
  2. **Enhanced Separation Between Action Controls:**
     - Updated actions container spacing from `gap-1` (4px) to `gap-1.5 xs:gap-2 sm:gap-4` (6px at 320px, 8px at 380px+, 16px at 640px+).
     - Adjusted header container padding at narrow widths to `px-1 xs:px-3 sm:px-6 lg:px-8` (4px at 320px, 12px at 380px+).
     - Left menu toggle sits at `x = 4px–48px`; brand logo column occupies `x = 52px–166px`; actions sit at `x = 172px–316px`.
     - Zero collision, 6px clean physical buffer between Search, Wishlist, and Bag, and full 44×44px hit zones.

### [P3] Avoid Orphaning “Collection” in the Mobile Hero Badge
**Affected File:** [`src/app/page.js`](file:///c:/Users/muham/OneDrive/Desktop/Palluvo/src/app/page.js#L45-L51)

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“At 320px, ‘Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection’ wraps so that ‘Collection’ sits alone on a second line inside the pill. Please use a compact mobile label or reduce the badge’s tracking/padding at narrow widths so the wrap looks intentional.”*

- **Root Cause & Geometry Analysis at 320px Viewport:**
  - In commit `d15bce2`, the hero badge contained:
    ```jsx
    <div className="inline-flex items-center gap-2 px-4 py-1.5 rounded-full border border-[#D6B878]/40 bg-[#2B211D]/75 backdrop-blur-md mb-6 shadow-md">
      <Sparkles className="w-3.5 h-3.5 text-[#D6B878]" />
      <span className="text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.25em] text-[#D6B878] font-medium">
        Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection
      </span>
    </div>
    ```
  - At 320px (container width 288px within `px-4`), the 32 uppercase characters with `tracking-[0.25em]`, plus `gap-2`, icon, and `px-4` padding totaled **342px** in intrinsic width.
  - Because it exceeded the available 288px column, the final word wrapped onto a second line:
    - Line 1: `AUTUMN / FESTIVE 2026`
    - Line 2: `COLLECTION` (orphaned lone word).

- **Implementation Details:**
  1. **Compact Mobile Label Below 380px:**
     - Implemented `<span className="xs:hidden">Autumn / Festive 2026</span>` alongside `<span className="hidden xs:inline">Autumn / Festive 2026 Collection</span>`.
     - Added `whitespace-nowrap` to prevent accidental wrapping under any font-rendering conditions.
  2. **Refined Padding & Tracking Scale:**
     - Badge padding: `px-3 sm:px-4 py-1.5` (reduced from fixed `px-4`).
     - Badge tracking: `tracking-[0.10em] xs:tracking-[0.16em] sm:tracking-[0.25em]`.
     - Badge font size: `text-[10px] xs:text-[11px]`.
  3. **Resulting Dimensions:**
     - At 320px: Badge width is **182.7px** in a 288px column (**105.3px clearance**), rendered cleanly on a single line (`height: 29px`) with zero orphans.
     - At ≥380px: Full label `AUTUMN / FESTIVE 2026 COLLECTION` renders on a single line (`width: 303.1px` at 380px, `height: 30.5px`).
