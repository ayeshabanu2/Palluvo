### [P2] Keep the Secondary Hero CTA Clear of the Model at the 768px Breakpoint
**Affected File:** `src/app/page.js` (lines 44, 57, 62–75)

- **Reviewer Note:**
  - *“In src/app/page.js:62–75, md:flex-row turns on at 768px. At 767px the CTAs stack inside the text column; at 768px they move side-by-side, and ‘Explore Top Models’ extends over the model’s green sari. Please keep the CTAs stacked or otherwise reserve a clear text area until the image has enough space, and verify 768–1023px.”*

- **Root Cause & Layout Analysis:**
  - Previously, `md:flex-row` placed the primary and secondary CTAs horizontally side-by-side starting at 768px.
  - At 768px, the primary CTA (~300px) and secondary CTA (~230px) side-by-side with gap extended to x = 550px (71.6% across the 768px viewport), crossing into the model's emerald green saree and handloom pallu on the right.

- **Resolution:**
  1. **Maintained Vertical Stacking through 1023px (`lg:flex-row`):**
     Updated the hero CTA container in `src/app/page.js:62` to:
     ```jsx
     <div className="flex flex-col lg:flex-row items-start lg:items-center gap-3.5 sm:gap-4 max-w-xs sm:max-w-sm lg:max-w-none">
       <Link
         href="/sarees"
         className="w-full sm:w-auto max-w-xs sm:max-w-none bg-[#641C2D] hover:bg-[#7A3043] text-white px-7 sm:px-8 py-3.5 sm:py-4 rounded-full text-xs font-semibold tracking-[0.18em] uppercase transition shadow-2xl flex items-center justify-center gap-2 border border-[#8B1E2B] whitespace-nowrap min-h-[48px]"
       >
         Shop Curated Collection <ArrowRight className="w-4 h-4" />
       </Link>
       <a
         href="#signature-models"
         className="w-full sm:w-auto max-w-xs sm:max-w-none bg-white/10 hover:bg-white/20 text-[#EDE3D5] hover:text-white px-7 sm:px-8 py-3.5 sm:py-4 rounded-full text-xs font-semibold tracking-[0.18em] uppercase transition backdrop-blur-sm border border-white/20 flex items-center justify-center whitespace-nowrap min-h-[48px]"
       >
         Explore Top Models
       </a>
     </div>
     ```
  2. **Protected Left Column Width:** Updated the text column container to `md:max-w-md lg:max-w-2xl` so the hero copy stays within a protected corridor of 448px, preventing text from encroaching on the model.
  3. **Preserved Responsive Side-by-Side on Desktop (`lg:` 1024px+):** At 1024px and wider, `lg:flex-row` activates where the viewport provides ample horizontal space and the model is anchored `lg:object-right`.

- **In-Browser Verification Across 768–1023px:**
